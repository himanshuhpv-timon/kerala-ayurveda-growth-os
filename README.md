### Executive Overview
An enterprise-grade, automated analytics engine and leadership MBR dashboard built for Kerala Ayurveda Limited's Amazon India marketplace operations (Vendor Central 1P & Seller Central 3P).

### Features
1. **Shapley MECE Funnel Decomposition:** Exact mathematical attribution across Traffic, Conversion Rate, and ASP with 0.00% residual gap.
2. **Calendar vs. Velocity Run-Rate:** Isolates calendar day count effects (30 vs 31 days) from underlying commercial selling velocity.
3. **Advertising Efficiency & Cross-Channel Cannibalization:** Identifies duplicate 3P mirror campaigns bidding on 1P Buy Box ASINs at sub-optimal ROAS.
4. **Supply Chain Diagnostic:** Audits fulfillment center out-of-stock days on hero catalog items (Mahanarayana, Dhanwantharam, etc.).
5. **Interactive Leadership Target Simulator:** Customize MoM growth targets (+15%, +20%, +29%), ad spend policies, and TACoS ceilings with instant dynamic recalculation.
6. **Multi-Month Ingestion Pipeline:** Ingests raw CSV exports to compile fresh MBR reports and turnaround models for any historical or future cycle on the fly.
